# Pocket Tennis Color: TOURNAMENT, NEW GAME, ALICE, then lose the first match
# by serving with B over and over. The game sets up its save sector on first
# boot; starting the tournament and losing the match rewrite it, and the game
# returns to the title, where B does nothing. TOURNAMENT -> CONTINUE then shows
# the tournament.
# NGP A is libretro b, NGP B is libretro a, Option is start.
wait 1500
press b 6           # skip the intro
wait 200
press down 10
wait 60
press b 10          # TOURNAMENT
wait 200
press b 10          # NEW GAME
wait 200
press b 10          # ALICE
wait 200
press b 10          # past the draw
wait 600
tap a 600 30        # serve and swing with B until the match is lost
wait 300
shot title
press down 10
wait 60
press b 10          # TOURNAMENT
wait 200
press down 10
wait 60
press b 10          # CONTINUE
wait 300
shot continue
