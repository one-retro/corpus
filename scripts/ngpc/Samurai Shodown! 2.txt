# Samurai Shodown! 2: GAME OPTION, CPU LEVEL NORMAL -> HARD, EXIT, SAVE? YES. The game sets
# up its save block on first boot and keeps a PLAYTIME counter in it; leaving
# GAME OPTION with a save rewrites it. A reset and a second look at GAME OPTION shows HARD
# kept.
# NGP A is libretro b, NGP B is libretro a, Option is start.
wait 900
press b 6           # skip the intro
wait 300
repeat 4
press down 10       # GAME OPTION
wait 40
end
press b 10
wait 300
press right 10      # CPU LEVEL NORMAL -> HARD
wait 40
repeat 7
press down 10
wait 40
end
press b 10          # EXIT
wait 200
press b 10          # SAVE? YES: the options are written here
wait 300
shot exited
reset
wait 900
press b 6
wait 300
repeat 4
press down 10
wait 40
end
press b 10          # GAME OPTION again
wait 300
press down 10       # off CPU LEVEL
wait 40
shot kept
