# Neo Turf Masters: GAME OPTIONS -> GAME LEVEL, NORMAL -> HARD, then back out
# to the menu. The game keeps its save in a whole 64 KiB sector (0x3E0000),
# set up on first boot and rewritten with the new level. A reset and a second
# look at GAME LEVEL shows HARD kept.
# NGP A is libretro b, NGP B is libretro a, Option is start.
wait 1100
press b 6           # skip the intro: PRESS BUTTON A
wait 300
press b 6           # 1 PLAYER / VS MODE PLAY / GAME OPTIONS
wait 300
press down 10
wait 60
press down 10
wait 60
press b 10          # GAME OPTIONS
wait 300
press b 10          # GAME LEVEL
wait 200
press down 10       # NORMAL -> HARD
wait 60
press b 10          # choose it: the options are written here
wait 200
press a 10          # back to the menu
wait 200
shot set
reset
wait 1100
press b 6
wait 300
press b 6
wait 300
press down 10
wait 60
press down 10
wait 60
press b 10          # GAME OPTIONS
wait 300
press b 10          # GAME LEVEL again
wait 200
shot kept
