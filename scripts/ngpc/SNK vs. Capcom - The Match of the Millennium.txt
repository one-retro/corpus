# SNK vs. Capcom - The Match of the Millennium (32 Mbit, two flash chips; the
# save lives in the first chip's boot sectors): SET UP, LEVEL NORMAL -> HARD,
# EXIT. Leaving SET UP rewrites the save area. A reset and a second look at
# SET UP shows LEVEL HARD kept.
# NGP A is libretro b, NGP B is libretro a, Option is start.
wait 1250           # the intro runs into the title, PRESS "A" BUTTON
press b 6           # START / SET UP
wait 200
press down 10       # SET UP
wait 60
press b 10
wait 250
press right 10      # LEVEL NORMAL -> HARD
wait 40
repeat 6
press down 10
wait 30
end
press b 10          # EXIT: the settings are written here
wait 150
shot exited
reset
wait 1250
press b 6
wait 200
press down 10
wait 60
press b 10          # SET UP again
wait 150
press down 10       # off LEVEL
wait 40
shot kept
