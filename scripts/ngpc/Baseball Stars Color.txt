# Baseball Stars Color: GAME OPTION, change INNING and CALLED, EXIT. Leaving the
# options writes them to flash (the game alternates between two 8 KiB
# sectors). A reset and a second look at GAME OPTION shows them kept.
# NGP A is libretro b, NGP B is libretro a, Option is start.
wait 900
press b 6           # skip the intro
wait 400
press down 10       # GAME OPTION (2P PLAY is skipped without a link cable)
wait 40
press b 10
wait 150
press right 10      # INNING
wait 40
press down 10
wait 40
press right 10      # CALLED ON -> OFF
wait 40
repeat 3
press down 10
wait 40
end
press b 10          # EXIT: the options are written here
wait 150
shot exited
reset
wait 900
press b 6
wait 400
press down 10
wait 40
press b 10          # GAME OPTION again
wait 150
shot kept
