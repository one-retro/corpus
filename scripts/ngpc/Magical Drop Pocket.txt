# Magical Drop Pocket: OPTIONS, Total Games 3 -> 5, Exit. The game sets up its
# save block on first boot; leaving the options rewrites it. A reset and a
# second look at OPTIONS shows the new value kept.
# NGP A is libretro b, NGP B is libretro a, Option is start.
wait 1300
press b 6           # skip the intro: STORY / SELF CHALLENGE / ...
wait 300
press up 10         # OPTIONS (the cursor wraps)
wait 60
press b 10
wait 200
press right 10      # Total Games 3 -> 5
wait 40
repeat 6
press down 10
wait 40
end
press b 10          # Exit: the options are written here
wait 200
shot exited
reset
wait 1300
press b 6
wait 300
press up 10
wait 60
press b 10          # OPTIONS again
wait 200
press down 10       # off Total Games
wait 40
shot kept
