# Bust-A-Move Pocket: GAME OPTIONS, GAME-LEVEL LV 1 -> LV 2, EXIT. The game sets
# up its save block (a whole 64 KiB sector) on first boot; leaving the options
# rewrites it. A reset and a second look at GAME OPTIONS shows LV 2 kept.
# NGP A is libretro b, NGP B is libretro a, Option is start.
wait 900
press b 6           # skip the intro: GAME MENU
wait 300
press up 10         # GAME OPTIONS (the cursor wraps)
wait 60
press b 10
wait 200
press right 10      # GAME-LEVEL LV 1 -> LV 2
wait 40
repeat 5
press down 10
wait 40
end
press b 10          # EXIT: the options are written here
wait 200
shot exited
reset
wait 900
press b 6
wait 300
press up 10
wait 60
press b 10          # GAME OPTIONS again
wait 200
press down 10       # off GAME-LEVEL
wait 40
shot kept
