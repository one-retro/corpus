# Metal Slug - 1st Mission: SET UP, LEVEL NORMAL -> HARD, EXIT. The game sets
# up its save block (a whole 64 KiB sector, 0x3E0000) on first boot; leaving
# SET UP rewrites it. A reset and a second look at SET UP shows LEVEL kept.
# NGP A is libretro b, NGP B is libretro a, Option is start.
wait 600
press b 6           # skip the intro: PRESS A BUTTON
wait 300
press b 6           # START / SET UP
wait 300
press down 10       # SET UP
wait 60
press b 10
wait 200
press right 10      # LEVEL NORMAL -> HARD
wait 40
repeat 4
press down 10
wait 40
end
press b 10          # EXIT: the settings are written here
wait 200
shot exited
reset
wait 600
press b 6
wait 300
press b 6
wait 300
press down 10
wait 60
press b 10          # SET UP again
wait 200
press down 10       # off LEVEL
wait 40
shot kept
