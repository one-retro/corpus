# King of Fighters R-2: SET UP, LEVEL NORMAL -> HARD, EXIT. The game sets up
# its save area on first boot; leaving SET UP rewrites it with the new level.
# A reset and a second look at SET UP shows LEVEL HARD kept.
# NGP A is libretro b, NGP B is libretro a, Option is start.
wait 1000
press b 6           # skip the intro
wait 200
press down 10       # SET UP
wait 60
press b 10
wait 150
press right 10      # LEVEL NORMAL -> HARD
wait 40
repeat 6
press down 10
wait 30
end
press b 10          # EXIT: the settings are written here
wait 150
shot exited
reset
wait 1000
press b 6
wait 200
press down 10
wait 60
press b 10          # SET UP again
wait 150
press down 10       # off LEVEL, so it stops blinking
wait 40
shot kept
