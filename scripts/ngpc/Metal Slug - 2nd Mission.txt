# Metal Slug - 2nd Mission (32 Mbit, two flash chips): SET UP, LEVEL NORMAL ->
# HARD, EXIT. Leaving SET UP writes the settings to flash. A reset and a second
# look at SET UP shows LEVEL kept.
# NGP A is libretro b, NGP B is libretro a, Option is start.
wait 2250           # the intro runs into the title, PRESS A BUTTON
press b 6           # START / SET UP
wait 300
press down 10       # SET UP
wait 60
press b 10
wait 200
press right 10      # LEVEL NORMAL -> HARD
wait 40
repeat 4
press down 10
wait 40
end
shot setup
press b 10          # EXIT: the settings are written here
wait 200
shot exited
reset
wait 2250
press b 6
wait 300
press down 10
wait 60
press b 10          # SET UP again
wait 200
press down 10       # off LEVEL
wait 40
shot kept
