# Tekken Card Challenge (SRAM 8 KiB). Start an ADVENTURE: the game shows the
# owner profile from the console's internal EEPROM (Beetle synthesises it:
# NAME = frontend user name, BIRTHDAY 19890623, FEMALE, blood O), then JIN,
# STAGE 01. The game saves the adventure as soon as it starts. After a reset,
# ADVENTURE lists the saved JIN Lv 1 STAGE01.
wait 900
press start 6       # title
wait 120
press a 6           # menu: アドベンチャー (adventure)
wait 120
press a 6           # owner profile screen
wait 120
press a 6           # CHARACTER SELECT: JIN
wait 120
press a 6           # JIN's story text
wait 120
tap a 6 40          # into STAGE 01
shot stage1
reset
wait 900
press start 6       # (the attract demo's VS screen)
wait 120
press a 6           # title
wait 120
press a 6           # menu
wait 120
press a 6           # アドベンチャー: the saved character
wait 120
shot continue       # CHARACTER SELECT shows JIN Lv 1 STAGE01
