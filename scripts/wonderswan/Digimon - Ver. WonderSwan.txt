# Digimon - Ver. WonderSwan (HK, English; SRAM 32 KiB). A virtual-pet game.
# REBOOT starts a new pet on the network map; SYSTEM > SAVE&EXIT writes the
# save and returns to the title (264 bytes differ from the SRAM at that point;
# the game also initialises ~1.6 KiB of SRAM on its first boot).
wait 1000
press start 6       # title menu: REBOOT
wait 120
press a 6           # REBOOT -> network map, cursor on SYSTEM
wait 300
press a 6           # SYSTEM: CANCEL / TIME JUMP / MAIL / CLOCK / SAVE&EXIT
wait 200
repeat 4
press down 8
wait 30
end
shot menu
press a 6           # SAVE&EXIT
wait 120
shot saved          # back at the title after saving
