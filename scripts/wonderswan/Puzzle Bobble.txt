# Puzzle Bobble (EEPROM 128 B). Portrait game: in this core's default keymap the
# Y pad is the d-pad, `l` = portrait down, `r` = up; A confirms.
# Play 1P puzzle mode, firing straight up until round 1 is lost (score 420).
# The game writes the result (round 1, score 420) to EEPROM; after a reset the
# "past results" screen reads it back. Boot alone leaves the EEPROM all zero.
wait 1000
press start 6       # title -> MODE SELECT
wait 120
press a 6           # 1人で遊ぼうかな (1 player)
wait 120
press a 6           # さいしょから (from the start) -> ROUND 1
wait 200
repeat 30
press a 6           # fire
wait 54
end
shot lost
reset
wait 1000
press start 6       # title -> MODE SELECT
wait 300
press l2 12         # the first input on this menu is dropped; l2 does nothing here
wait 30
repeat 3
press l 12          # down to 過去をふりかえる (past results)
wait 30
end
press a 6
wait 120
press a 6           # 1人で遊ぼうかな
wait 120
shot results        # これまでの結果: ラウンド 1, スコア 420
