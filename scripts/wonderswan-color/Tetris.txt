# Tetris (WSC, EEPROM 128 B). Portrait game. In this core's default keymap the
# d-pad drives the X pad: `down` (X3) confirms or changes a value, `left` (X4)
# goes back; `l`/`r` move the cursor down/up (Y pad). OPTION: set NEXT PIECE
# and GHOST PIECE to OFF and leave through EXIT, which writes the options
# (boot leaves the EEPROM all zero). After a reset OPTION shows them again.
wait 900
press l 12          # title: SINGLE PLAY / VS TETRIS / OPTION
wait 60
press l 12          # OPTION
wait 60
press down 12       # open OPTION
wait 120
press down 12       # NEXT PIECE 03 -> OFF
wait 60
press l 12
wait 60
press down 12       # GHOST PIECE ON -> OFF
wait 60
repeat 8
press l 12          # down past HOLD, BGM, SE, the rankings, CREDITS to EXIT
wait 40
end
press down 12       # EXIT: the options are written
wait 120
reset
wait 900
press l 12
wait 60
press l 12
wait 60
press down 12       # OPTION again
wait 120
shot options        # NEXT PIECE OFF, GHOST PIECE OFF, read back from EEPROM
