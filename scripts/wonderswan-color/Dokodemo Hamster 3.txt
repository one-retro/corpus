# Dokodemo Hamster 3 - Odekake Saffron (SRAM 32 KiB, RTC cart). はじめから, then
# hold A through the setup: owner name (あ...), birthday, blood type, today's
# date and time (left at 2000-01-01 0:00), hamster name (あ...). The game saves
# when the hamster arrives in its room. After a reset the title adds つづきから,
# which loads the room. Beetle keeps the cart clock itself; the .srm is SRAM only.
wait 900
press start 8       # title -> はじめから
wait 150
press a 8           # はじめから
wait 150
tap a 36 40         # owner name, birthday, blood type, date/time, hamster intro
tap a 30 40         # hamster name, closing dialogue -> the room
wait 600
shot room
reset
wait 900
press start 8       # title: はじめから / つづきから
wait 150
shot menu           # つづきから appears only with a save
press a 8           # つづきから
wait 150
shot continued      # the hamster's room
