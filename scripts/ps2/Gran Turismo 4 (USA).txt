# Gran Turismo 4 (USA), SCUS-97328, DVD.
# At power-on: "No GT4 game data found ... create game data? OK" > NAME ENTRY (the
# cursor starts on Enter; Left reaches L; after the first capital the keyboard turns
# lower case, so three presses type "Lll") > Enter > "proceed with this name? OK" >
# "Game data will now be created. OK" > "1498KB of free space is required. OK" >
# "unformatted. Format? Cancel/OK" (Cancel preselected: Right, then OK). The game
# formats the card and writes its 1.5 MB game data: the largest save here.
wait 1500
shot create          # "No GT4 game data found ... Do you wish to create game data?" (OK)
press b 10
wait 400
press left 10        # Enter -> L
wait 40
press b 10           # L
wait 40
press b 10           # l
wait 40
press b 10           # l
wait 40
press right 10       # -> Enter
wait 40
press b 10
wait 400
shot name            # "The name entered is: Lll. Proceed?" (OK)
press b 10
wait 400
press b 10           # "Game data will now be created." OK
wait 600
shot space           # "1498KB of free space is required" (OK)
press b 10
wait 600
shot format          # "currently unformatted. Format?" (Cancel selected)
press right 10       # OK
wait 60
press b 10
wait 1800
shot saved           # "Save successful"
