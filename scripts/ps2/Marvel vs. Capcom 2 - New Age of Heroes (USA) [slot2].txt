# Marvel vs. Capcom 2 - New Age of Heroes (USA), SLUS-20486, CD. Slot 2 variant: the same
# save, made on the card in MEMORY CARD slot 2 (filed as "<game> [slot2]").
# Main menu > SAVE/LOAD > SAVE > slot 2; the game offers to format an unformatted card
# (the PCSX2-based cores create cards unformatted), then writes BASLUS-20486SYS.
wait 1800
press start 6        # skip the intro to the title
wait 300
press start 6        # title -> main menu (ARCADE MODE selected)
wait 300
press down 6         # SCORE RANKING
wait 30
press right 6        # SCORE ATTACK
wait 30
press right 6        # SECRET FACTOR
wait 30
press right 6        # SAVE/LOAD
wait 60
press b 6            # open SAVE/LOAD
wait 120
press b 6            # SAVE
wait 120
press down 6         # SLOT 2
wait 30
press b 6            # SLOT 2
wait 120
shot format
press left 6         # "unformatted. Will you format it?" -> Yes
wait 30
press b 6
wait 300
shot saved           # "Save successful. Autosaved data will be saved to ... slot2."
