# Max Payne 2 - The Fall of Max Payne (USA), SLUS-20814, DVD.
# At power-on the game asks to format the unformatted card, then creates its whole
# save folder by itself ("Creating savegame folder"): options plus six empty 150 KiB
# save slots and three icons, about 1.1 MiB.
wait 5000
shot prompt          # "... is unformatted. Do you wish to format ...? Yes / No"
press up 10          # Yes
wait 60
press b 10
wait 300
shot creating        # "Creating savegame folder."
wait 900
shot menu            # main menu, Load now enabled
