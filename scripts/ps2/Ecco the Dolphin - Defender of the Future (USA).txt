# Ecco the Dolphin - Defender of the Future (USA), SLUS-20394, DVD.
# Player Menu > (no Ecco data) create > format the card > NEW GAME > initials "AAA":
# the game writes its folder with a player file ECCO_AAA.
# The intro (logos, title, attract demo) cycles without input; START only opens the menu while
# the title shows, 1500-2400 frames after the language choice in PCEE2 and Play! but
# 2400-4800 in LRPS2, hence the per-core variant .lrps2.txt (only that wait differs).
wait 3000
shot language
press b 10           # LANGUAGE: ENGLISH
wait 2000
shot title
press start 10       # title -> menu
wait 400
press down 10
wait 40
press down 10        # PLAYER MENU
wait 40
press b 10
wait 300
press b 10           # "does not contain Ecco data ... press X to create"
wait 300
shot format
press a 10           # "unformatted. Format?" YES = Circle
wait 800
press b 10           # NEW GAME
wait 300
press b 10           # A
wait 300
press b 10           # A
wait 300
press start 10
wait 300
press b 10           # A -> AAA
wait 300
press b 10
wait 300
shot player          # main menu, "PLAYER: AAA"
