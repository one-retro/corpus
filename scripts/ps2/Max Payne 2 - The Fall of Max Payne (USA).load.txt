# Max Payne 2 - The Fall of Max Payne (USA), SLUS-20814, DVD: show a loaded save.
# With BASLUS-20814MaxPay2 on the card the game says "Autoload successful." at power-on (with
# none it asks to create the folder). Continue > main menu (How to Play ...). Load lists the
# card's savegames (date, part, chapter); New Game > SELECT GAME MODE greys out the modes the
# save has not unlocked (with no save only Detective is white).
wait 4500            # "Autoload successful." waits for input (at ~3500 frames in PCEE2, ~4000 in LRPS2)
shot autoload        # "Autoload successful."
press b 10           # Continue
wait 600
press down 10        # Resume Game
wait 40
press down 10        # New Game
wait 40
press down 10        # Load
wait 40
press b 10
wait 600
shot load            # LOAD MENU: savegames
press x 10           # back
wait 400
press up 10          # New Game
wait 40
press b 10
wait 600
shot modes           # SELECT GAME MODE: unlocked modes in white
