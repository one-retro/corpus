# Deep Dungeon II - Yuushi no Monshou: register "ABC" (なまえ とうろく, no
# password), then start the game with it. The roster reaches the 8 KiB SRAM
# only when the game starts: a reset straight after registering shows an
# empty roster. Joystick only: fMSX runs with --device0 257.
# The reset is the power cycle (blueMSX writes its .sram only then); after it
# the roster shows ABC again.
wait 900
press a 8           # title -> roster
wait 150
repeat 5
press down 6        # -> なまえ とうろく (register)
wait 20
end
press a 8           # -> slot list
wait 150
press a 8           # slot 1 -> パスワードをいれますか? (enter a password?)
wait 200
press down 8        # いいえ (no)
wait 60
press a 8           # -> name grid, cursor on あ
wait 200
repeat 5
press down 6
wait 20
end
press right 6       # -> A
wait 20
press a 6
wait 40
press right 6
wait 20
press a 6           # B
wait 40
press right 6
wait 20
press a 6           # C
wait 40
press b 8           # -> おわり (done)
wait 60
press a 8
wait 200
press a 8           # start with ABC -> かみのことば (god's words)
wait 300
press a 8           # -> overworld
wait 300
shot started
reset
wait 900
press a 8
wait 200
shot roster
