# Dance Dance Revolution Konamix: at boot the game finds no system data and
# offers to create it. Answering YES writes "KONAMIX SYSTEM", a 1-block save,
# about 30 seconds after power-on.
wait 2400           # "No system data. Do you wish to create system data?" (cursor on NO)
press left 6        # YES
wait 30
shot ask
press b 6           # "Saving... Do not remove MEMORY CARD."
wait 360
shot saved          # "Saved system data."
wait 60
