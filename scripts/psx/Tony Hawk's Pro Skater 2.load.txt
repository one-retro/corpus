# Tony Hawk's Pro Skater 2: a THPS2 card holds careers, parks and replays, each listed in its
# own screen. Options > Memory Card > Load Data lists the careers (shot `career`), Load Replay the
# replays (shot `replay`); Park Editor > Load lists the parks (shot `park`).
wait 1500           # BIOS logos
repeat 2            # skip the intros to the main menu
press start 6
wait 250
end
wait 600            # the game loads the last career at boot
press left 6        # OPTIONS
wait 60
press b 6
wait 200
repeat 4            # MEMORY CARD (a career with cheats adds CHEATS below it, so count from the top;
press down 6        # SwanStation greys out Player 2 Controls: its variant presses 3)
wait 30
end
press b 6
wait 300
press b 6           # LOAD DATA
wait 400
shot career
press x 6
wait 300
press down 6
wait 30
press down 6        # LOAD REPLAY
wait 30
press b 6
wait 400
shot replay
press x 6
wait 200
press x 6
wait 200
press x 6           # back to the main menu, on OPTIONS
wait 300
press left 6        # PARK EDITOR
wait 60
press b 6
wait 1800
press b 6           # past the layout tips
wait 300
press start 6       # the editor's menu
wait 200
repeat 5            # LOAD
press down 6
wait 30
end
press b 6
wait 400
shot park
