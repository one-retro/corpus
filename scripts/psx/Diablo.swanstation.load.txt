# Diablo, swanstation variant: two kinds of save. A game (BASLUS-00619-DIAB-01..) is named on Load Game > Select Memory
# Card (shot `load`); a "saved characters" file (-DIAB-88) is listed by New Game > One player >
# Load Character > slot 1 (shot `character`).
wait 1500           # BIOS logos
repeat 5            # SwanStation shows the menu after five presses
press start 6
wait 250
end
press down 6        # LOAD GAME
wait 30
press b 6
wait 400
shot load
press x 6           # back
wait 200
press up 6          # NEW GAME
wait 30
press b 6
wait 300
press b 6           # ONE player
wait 300
press down 6
wait 30
press down 6
wait 30
press down 6        # LOAD CHARACTER
wait 30
press b 6
wait 400
press b 6           # memory card slot 1
wait 400
shot character
