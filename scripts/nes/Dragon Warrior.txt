# Dragon Warrior: begin a new quest as "ABC". The game writes the new
# Adventure Log (ten copies, flag 0xC8 at 0x035) to battery RAM as soon as the
# name and message speed are chosen. After the King's opening speech the
# console is reset, and CONTINUE A QUEST lists the log, read back from SRAM.
wait 500
press start 20      # title (the press lands once the title settles)
wait 360
press start 20
wait 60
press a 20          # BEGIN A NEW QUEST
wait 90
tap a 1 20          # A (cursor starts on A)
tap right 1 10
tap a 1 20          # B
tap right 1 10
tap a 1 20          # C
tap down 5 10       # bottom row
tap right 5 10      # END
tap a 1 30
wait 60
tap up 1 10         # message speed: FAST
tap a 1 30
wait 120
tap a 30 30         # the King's speech
press b 6
wait 60
shot throne
reset
wait 500
press start 20
wait 360
press start 20
wait 90
shot menu
press a 6           # CONTINUE A QUEST
wait 40
shot continue       # the log list: ADVENTURE LOG 1 ABC
wait 30
