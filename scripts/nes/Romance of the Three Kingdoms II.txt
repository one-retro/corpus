# Romance of the Three Kingdoms II (USA): MMC5, 32 KiB battery WRAM.
# Koei's prompts: numbers change with up/down, (Y/N) answers with LEFT = Y, RIGHT = N; A does not answer them.
# Scenario 1, one player as Yuan Shu (province 19), level 1; on his first turn SELECT -> 3.SAVE -> Y.
# Random events differ between cores, hence the B/A pairs below.
# Proof: after a reset, 7.Load Saved Game brings back Yuan Shu's turn in Jan 189.
wait 5400             # the intro runs into the title; PUSH START stays up
press start
wait 600
shot scenarios
press a               # 1. Dong Zhuo Seizes Loyang
wait 600
press right           # players: 00 -> 01 (the cursor starts on the tens digit)
wait 60
press up
wait 60
shot players
press a
wait 600
press a               # the cursor starts on province 19: Yuan Shu
wait 600
press left            # Yuan Shu OK? Y
wait 600
press up              # game level 0 -> 1
wait 60
press a
wait 600
press right           # View other wars? N
wait 600
shot ok
press left            # Everything OK? Y
wait 4000             # events and the other rulers' turns, then Yuan Shu's orders
shot turn
repeat 2              # a random event may ask first (e.g. an alliance letter: 1.Accept): B leaves it
press b               # alone and A accepts; at the orders menu B shows the map and A brings the menu
wait 400              # back. Either way the orders menu is up afterwards.
press a
wait 400
end
press select          # Now: 1.WAIT 2.HEX 3.SAVE
wait 300
press down
wait 60
press down
wait 60
press a               # 3.SAVE
wait 400
shot save
press left            # Save game? Y
wait 900
shot quit
press right           # Quit game? N
wait 600
shot saved
reset
wait 5400
press start
wait 600
repeat 6
press down
wait 40
end
shot load
press a               # 7. Load Saved Game
wait 1800
shot loaded
