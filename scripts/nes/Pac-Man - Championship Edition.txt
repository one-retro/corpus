# with 8 KiB battery WRAM ("sav" block at 0x700).
# One NORMAL game with no input: Pac-Man eats a few dots (40 points) and loses his three lives. The
# game keeps the result and a replay of the play in battery RAM.
# Proof: after a reset, OPTIONS -> REPLAY NORMAL plays that game back (REPLAY, 1UP 0000040).

press start           # title -> NORMAL / EXTRA / OPTIONS
wait 600
press start           # NORMAL
wait 6000             # three lives lost, RESULTS
shot results
press a
wait 400
press a
wait 400
press start           # back to the title
wait 600
reset
wait 900
press start
wait 600
press down
wait 60
press down
wait 60
press a               # OPTIONS
wait 600
press down
wait 60
press down
wait 60
shot options          # REPLAY NORMAL lit, REPLAY EXTRA dim
press a               # REPLAY NORMAL
