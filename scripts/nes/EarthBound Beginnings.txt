# EarthBound Beginnings (MMC3, 8 KiB): start GAME(1), take the default
# names (each "A", Start ends a name), and let the prologue run into Ninten's
# room: a new game saves itself to GAME(1) (0x1700) once play begins. The
# console is then reset and the file menu, read from SRAM, lists GAME(1).
wait 600
repeat 16
press start 10      # title, GAME(1), names and food, then the prologue
wait 60
press a 10
wait 60
end
repeat 10
wait 300            # the prologue, "80 years have passed", Ninten's room
press a 6
end
press b 10
wait 60
press b 10
wait 120
shot room
reset
wait 300
repeat 8
press start 10      # the title takes Start only once its logo has settled
wait 60
end
shot files0
wait 60
shot files
wait 30
