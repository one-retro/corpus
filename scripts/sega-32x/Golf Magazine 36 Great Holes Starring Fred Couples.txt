# Golf Magazine Presents 36 Great Holes Starring Fred Couples (32X): Player
# Edit -> NEW PLAYER, keep the defaults, back out with B. The new golfer is
# written to the cart's byte-wide SRAM (header 200001-20FFFF) and shows up as
# a third entry, "NEW PLAYER", under Fred Couples.
# Genesis A/B/C are the RetroPad's y/b/a.
wait 2100
press a 6           # title ("Press Start" also takes C) -> main menu
wait 150
press down 6
wait 40
press down 6        # Player Edit
wait 40
press a 6           # "Choose a player to edit..."
wait 300
press a 6           # NEW PLAYER -> Player Attributes
wait 300
shot attributes
press b 6           # back: the player is created and saved
wait 300
shot saved          # NEW PLAYER, FRED COUPLES, NEW PLAYER
wait 30
