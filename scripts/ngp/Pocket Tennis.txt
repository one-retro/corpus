# Pocket Tennis: a TOURNAMENT match, lost by serving with B over and over. When
# the match is lost the game writes the record to flash and returns to the
# title, where B does nothing. RECORD then shows the tournament played.
# NGP A is libretro b, NGP B is libretro a, Option is start.
wait 1700           # the intro runs into the title menu
press down 10
wait 40
press b 10          # TOURNAMENT
wait 150
press b 10          # NEW GAME
wait 150
press b 10          # player ROSE
wait 150
press b 10          # past the draw
wait 600
tap a 500 30        # serve and swing with B until the match is lost
wait 300
shot title
press down 10
wait 40
press down 10
wait 40
press b 10          # RECORD
wait 200
shot record
