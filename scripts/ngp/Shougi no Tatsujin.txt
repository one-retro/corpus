# Shougi no Tatsujin: a game of honshougi at the first level; move the 5th
# pawn one square, let the computer answer, then Option -> セーブ／ロード, A
# saves the position. After a reset, a new game, Option, right to ロード and A
# loads it back.
# NGP A is libretro b, NGP B is libretro a, Option is start.
wait 400
press b 6           # skip the intro
wait 200
press b 10          # title
wait 200
press b 10          # 本将棋
wait 200
press b 10          # 初心者
wait 200
press b 10          # opponent
wait 200
tap b 2 40          # past the greeting
press down 10       # cursor onto the 5th pawn
wait 40
press b 10
wait 60
press up 10
wait 40
press b 10          # pawn forward
wait 600            # the computer answers
press start 10      # セーブ／ロード
wait 100
press b 10          # A: save
wait 200
shot saved
reset
wait 400
press b 6
wait 200
press b 10
wait 200
press b 10
wait 200
press b 10
wait 200
press b 10
wait 200
tap b 2 40
press start 10      # セーブ／ロード
wait 100
press right 10      # ロード
wait 40
press b 10          # A: load
wait 300
shot loaded
