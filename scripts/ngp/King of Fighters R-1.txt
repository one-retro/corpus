# King of Fighters R-1: SET UP, LEVEL NORMAL -> HARD, EXIT. Leaving SET UP
# writes the settings (and the empty RECORD table) to flash. A reset and a
# second look at SET UP shows LEVEL HARD kept.
# NGP A is libretro b, NGP B is libretro a, Option is start.
wait 400
press b 6           # skip the intro
wait 200
press down 10       # SET UP
wait 60
press b 10
wait 120
press right 10      # LEVEL NORMAL -> HARD
wait 40
repeat 7
press down 10
wait 30
end
press b 10          # EXIT: the settings are written here
wait 120
shot exited
reset
wait 400
press b 6
wait 200
press down 10
wait 60
press b 10          # SET UP again
wait 120
press down 10       # off LEVEL, so it stops blinking
wait 40
shot kept
