# Grizzards v1.1 (Bruce-Robert Pocock, 2022, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0): an RPG that
# needs a SaveKey/AtariVox and keeps three game slots on it (slot 1 at EEPROM
# 0x1100, "GRIZ0"). Begin slot 1, name the player BAAAAA, take Aquax and BEGIN:
# the new game is written to the SaveKey at once. Reset: Select Slot now offers
# "RESUME BAAAAA SLOT 1". Up/down change a letter, fire moves on.
wait 1200
press b 6           # title -> SELECT SLOT: BEGIN SLOT 1
wait 450
press b 6           # ENTER YOUR NAME
wait 300
press up 6          # A -> B
wait 30
repeat 6            # six letters: BAAAAA
press b 6
wait 60
end
wait 120            # SELECT AQUAX
press b 6
wait 150            # BAAAAA AQUAX: CHANGE / BEGIN
press down 6        # BEGIN
wait 30
press b 6           # the slot is written now
wait 600
shot begun          # the first room
reset
wait 1200
press b 6           # title -> SELECT SLOT
wait 450
shot saved          # RESUME BAAAAA SLOT 1
