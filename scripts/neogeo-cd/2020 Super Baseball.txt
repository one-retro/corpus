# 2020 Super Baseball: saves game data during play (the card changes at cycles 28 and 50).
# Neo Geo A is the RetroPad b. The script presses START and A over and over,
# which goes through the title, the menus and into play, until the game has
# saved (cycle 51 of the mashing; found by dumping the card after every cycle).
# A new card is unformatted: the game formats it itself before its first save.
# Then a reset brings up the BIOS CD player; its MEMORY button opens the
# backup memory utility, and "2" lists the data names on the card (the proof).
wait 900
repeat 51
press start 6
wait 60
press b 6
wait 90
press b 6
wait 90
end
wait 300
reset
wait 1000
press down 6        # CD player: the cursor starts on the eject button
wait 30
press down 6
wait 30
press right 6
wait 30
press right 6
wait 30
press down 6        # MEMORY
wait 30
press b 6           # -> backup memory utility, cursor on 1 (format)
wait 240
press down 6        # 2: data name list
wait 30
press b 6
wait 200
shot memory
