# Stakes Winner: does not format a new card (an unformatted card stayed all zeros through 60
# cycles), so the card is formatted first in the BIOS. At power-on the front-loader BIOS shows its
# CD player; MEMORY opens the backup memory utility, whose "1" formats the card. "5" exits, and
# START on the CD player boots the disc. Then the script presses START and A over and over, which
# goes through the title, the menus and into play; the game writes its data in cycle 1 and keeps
# adding to it (cycles 3, 11, 24, 37, 50; found by dumping the card after every cycle).
# Neo Geo A is the RetroPad b. At the end a reset brings back the CD player, and the utility's "2"
# lists the data names on the card (the proof).
wait 1000
press down 6        # CD player: the cursor starts on the eject button
wait 30
press down 6
wait 30
press right 6
wait 30
press right 6
wait 30
press down 6        # MEMORY
wait 30
press b 6           # -> backup memory utility, cursor on 1 (format)
wait 120
press b 6           # format
wait 120
press b 6           # confirm
wait 200
repeat 4            # down to 5 (exit)
press down 6
wait 30
end
press b 6
wait 200
press start 6       # CD player: play the disc
wait 600
repeat 51
press start 6
wait 60
press b 6
wait 90
press b 6
wait 90
end
wait 300
reset
wait 1000
press down 6        # CD player: the cursor starts on the eject button
wait 30
press down 6
wait 30
press right 6
wait 30
press right 6
wait 30
press down 6        # MEMORY
wait 30
press b 6           # -> backup memory utility, cursor on 1 (format)
wait 240
press down 6        # 2: data name list
wait 30
press b 6
wait 200
shot memory
