# NFL Quarterback Club 96: Play -> New Season, take the Cowboys and the 1996
# schedule, and save the season to slot 1 ("COWBOYS 1996 W0-L0-T0"). Seasons
# live in the cart's 24C16 EEPROM (2 KB, Acclaim wiring). Back out to the Play
# menu and open Resume Season. (A reset instead leaves Genesis Plus GX
# on blue and black stripes.) In this game B selects, C goes back.
# Genesis A/B/C are the RetroPad's y/b/a.
wait 1500
press start 6       # title -> main menu (Play / Practice / Simulation)
wait 300
press a 6           # Play
wait 300
press down 6        # New Season
wait 60
press a 6
wait 480            # TEAM SELECT, SEASON PLAY: Cowboys
press a 6
wait 480            # SEASON SCHEDULE 1996
press a 6
wait 480            # SAVE: 1. <EMPTY>
shot slots
press a 6           # save to slot 1
wait 480
shot saved1         # 1. COWBOYS 1996 W0-L0-T0
press r 6           # C back: schedule, team select, Play menu
wait 400
press r 6
wait 400
press r 6
wait 400
press down 6        # New Season
wait 60
press right 6       # Resume Season
wait 60
press a 6
wait 480
shot saved          # RESTORE: 1. COWBOYS 1996 W0-L0-T0, read from the EEPROM
