# Generated from NBA Jam.txt for BlastEm's RetroPad layout (b=A a=B r=C y=X x=Y l=Z);
# the shared script uses y=A b=B a=C l=X x=Y r=Z. Same Genesis buttons.
# One change: BlastEm runs this (USA, Europe) cart as a 50 Hz PAL console, so
# the game ends ~15% sooner in frames; the B loop is 86 rounds, not 105.
# NBA Jam: Head to Head with initials for record keeping, then play the whole
# game (the CPU wins; B is pressed now and then to clear halftime). The game
# writes the player's record to its 24C02 EEPROM when the final stats are
# dismissed; the STATS panel that follows shows the new record.
# Genesis A/B/C are the RetroPad's y/b/a.
wait 1820
press start 20      # title: the menu appears
wait 60
press start 10      # HEAD TO HEAD
wait 120
press start 10      # "Enter your initials for record keeping?" YES
wait 90
press r 6           # first letter
wait 60
press right 6
wait 40
press r 6           # second letter
wait 90
press right 6
wait 40
press right 6
wait 40
press r 6           # third letter (the grid enters "BDD" with these timings)
wait 90
repeat 3
press r 6
wait 60
end
shot initials
press start 6       # team select (it also times out on its own)
wait 120
press start 6
wait 120
repeat 86           # four quarters; B clears the halftime report
wait 300
press a 6
end
wait 4000           # the game ends; FINAL GAME STATS waits for a button
shot final
press a 6           # dismiss: the record is written now
wait 600
shot saved          # STATS: the record for the initials
wait 30
