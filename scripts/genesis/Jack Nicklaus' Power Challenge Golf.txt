# Jack Nicklaus' Power Challenge Golf (Accolade): Stroke Play, one player,
# Create Club Member named "AB". The member list lives in the cart's 2 KB
# byte-wide SRAM, which the header declares unusually ("RA" F8 40, 200001-
# 2007FF: the 0x40 bit is set, as on EEPROM carts). The proof is the club's
# "Welcome to the Nicklaus Club!" once the member is created.
# Genesis A/B/C are the RetroPad's y/b/a.
wait 1300
press start 6       # title -> credits
wait 300
press start 6       # -> Skins / Stroke Play
wait 240
press start 6       # Stroke Play, One Player -> "Select Player 1"
wait 120
press down 6        # Create Club Member
wait 30
press b 6           # (B selects in these menus)
wait 120
press up 6          # Name: A
wait 30
press right 6
wait 30
press up 6
wait 30
press up 6          # Name: AB
wait 30
shot named
press y 6           # A: done with the name
wait 120
press y 6           # A: done with the options: the member is written
wait 120
shot saved          # "Welcome to the Nicklaus Club!"
wait 30
