# NHLPA Hockey '93: New Playoffs with 5-minute periods, then let the CPU play
# the first game of the series out (C is pressed every 5 s: it clears the
# intermissions, overtime, and picks Exit Game on the FINAL board). Leaving the
# game writes the playoff state to the X24C01 EEPROM (EA's wiring); the game
# then sits on the PLAYOFF STATS screen, which C does not leave. The opponent
# is random, so cores play different games of different lengths.
# Genesis A/B/C are the RetroPad's y/b/a.
wait 1350
press start 6      # title -> game setup
wait 300
press right 6      # Play Mode: New Playoffs
wait 60
repeat 3
press down 6
wait 30
end
press right 6      # Per. Length: 20 Minutes
wait 30
press right 6      # Per. Length: 5 Minutes
wait 30
shot setup
press start 6      # the playoff bracket
wait 600
press start 6      # Ron Barr's welcome
wait 600
repeat 220         # 66000 frames: three periods, any overtime, Exit Game
wait 300
press a 6
end
shot saved         # PLAYOFF STATS for the finished game
wait 30
