# Variant for cores that power the cartridge RAM on as zeros (picodrive,
# sms-plus-gx): Monopoly takes an all-zero RAM for a valid saved game and asks
# PLAY SAVED GAME / PLAY NEW GAME before the token screen. Restoring that
# "game" shows GAME RESTORED over an empty board (picodrive then crashes);
# this script picks PLAY NEW GAME, and skips the extra PLAY SAVED GAME line
# in MORE OPTIONS; otherwise it is the same as Monopoly.txt.
# Monopoly: two human players "A" and "B", no computer player, no time limit,
# roll for first turn, then MAIN MENU > MORE OPTIONS > SAVE GAME:
# "GAME SAVED. PRESS B1 TO CONTINUE". Button 2 (a) starts from the title and
# begins the game; button 1 (b) enters everything else. Menu moves are spaced
# 30 frames apart: closer presses skip a line.
wait 400
press a 10          # PRESS START BUTTON -> PLAY SAVED GAME / PLAY NEW GAME
wait 150
press down 6        # -> PLAY NEW GAME
wait 30
press b 6           # -> CHOOSE A TOKEN
wait 150
press b 6           # first token -> ENTER YOUR NAME
wait 120
press b 6           # A
wait 60
press a 6           # -> DONE
wait 60
press b 6           # DONE: player 1 "A"
wait 150
press right 6       # next token
wait 60
press b 6           # -> ENTER YOUR NAME
wait 150
press right 6       # -> B
wait 60
press b 6           # B
wait 60
press a 6           # -> DONE
wait 60
press b 6           # DONE: player 2 "B"
wait 250
press a 6           # B-2 begin: COMPUTER PLAYER? NO
wait 150
press b 6           # NO -> SET TIME LIMIT? NO
wait 150
press b 6           # NO -> ROLL DICE TO DECIDE WHO STARTS FIRST
wait 250
press b 6           # roll; the other player rolls, SUMMARY OF PLAYERS
wait 800
press b 6           # -> MAIN MENU, MENU OPEN TO (first player)
wait 250
repeat 6            # ROLL DICE ... DISPLAY DEEDS -> MORE OPTIONS
press down 6
wait 30
end
press b 6
wait 150
repeat 5            # SET SCROLL / TIMER / MUSIC / CONVERT PLAYER / PLAY SAVED GAME -> SAVE GAME
press down 6
wait 30
end
press b 6           # "GAME SAVED"
wait 120
shot saved
wait 30
