# Pokemon Stadium: OPTIONS -> SOUND set to MONO. The game formats its
# FlashRAM on the first boot and writes the setting when the options close.
# The script then resets and opens OPTIONS again to show MONO was read back.
# No Transfer Pak is plugged in ("Game Pak None" on all four ports).
# N64 buttons on the RetroPad: A = b, B = y, Z = l2, Start = start.
wait 1600
press start 6       # title -> Game Pak Check
wait 200
press b 6           # OK -> Please Select
wait 250
press down 6
wait 30
press right 6       # OPTIONS
wait 30
press b 6
wait 250
press b 6           # SOUND: STEREO -> MONO
wait 150
shot mono
tap down 3 20       # QUIT
wait 20
press b 6           # close the options: the setting is written here
wait 250
reset
wait 1600
press start 6
wait 200
press b 6
wait 250
press down 6
wait 30
press right 6
wait 30
press b 6           # OPTIONS again after the reset
wait 250
shot reloaded
