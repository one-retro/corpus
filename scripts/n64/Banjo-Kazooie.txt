# Banjo-Kazooie: start Game 1, sit through the intro (A advances the text),
# then pause outside Banjo's house and SAVE AND QUIT. The game writes the
# file to its 4 Kbit EEPROM ("GAME OVER" is how it says goodbye) and goes
# back to the title; the file select then shows Game 1 with its play time.
# The pause menu follows the analog stick, not the D-pad.
# N64 buttons on the RetroPad: A = b, B = y, Z = l2, Start = start.
wait 2400
press start 6       # logos -> title
wait 300
press b 6           # (the title ignores A; these only pass time)
wait 300
press b 6
wait 300
press b 6
wait 300
press start 6       # title -> Banjo's bed: GAME 1: EMPTY
wait 300
press start 6       # "PRESS A TO PLAY THE GAME OR Z TO ERASE"
wait 300
press b 6           # Game 1: the intro starts
wait 300
repeat 48           # the intro, about four minutes: keep pressing A
repeat 15
press b 4
wait 16
end
end
wait 120
press start 6       # pause outside the house
wait 100
stick 0 max 6
wait 30
stick 0 max 6       # SAVE AND QUIT
wait 30
press b 6           # -> ARE YOU SURE? A - YES, B - NO
wait 200
press b 6           # yes: the file is written here, then GAME OVER
wait 300
shot saved
wait 3600           # back through the logos and the attract scene
press start 6       # -> title
wait 900
press start 6       # Banjo's bed: GAME 1 now has a play time
wait 400
shot reloaded
