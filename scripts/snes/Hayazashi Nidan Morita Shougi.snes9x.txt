# Hayazashi Nidan Morita Shougi (ST011): the battery RAM is the ST011's own
# 4 KB of data RAM. 入門教室 -> 対局はじめ -> あなたが先手; play 7六歩 (the 7七
# pawn up one), let the computer answer, resign (SELECT -> 投了), then セーブ
# to slot 1 without a comment. The game will not save a game with no moves.
# The ロード list then shows "1 棋譜1 .入門".
# Variant for the snes9x cores: their high-level ST011 resigns at once after
# the first move ("あなたの勝ちです"), so there is no SELECT -> 投了; B
# dismisses the result and the same menu follows. The record has 1 move.
wait 1200           # Seta logo, title
press start 8
wait 400
press a 8           # 入門教室
wait 300
press a 8           # 対局はじめ
wait 300
press a 8           # あなたが先手
wait 900
press left 10
wait 40
press left 10
wait 40
press down 10
wait 40
press a 10          # pick up the 7七 pawn
wait 60
press up 10
wait 40
press a 10          # 7六歩
wait 2400           # the computer resigns
shot g0
press b 15
wait 300
press down 15       # 再現 / ロード / セーブ / ...
wait 60
press down 15
wait 60
press a 15          # セーブ
wait 200
press a 15          # slot 1
wait 300
press down 15
wait 60
press a 15          # コメントしますか -> いいえ: saved to slot 1
wait 600
press up 15
wait 60
press a 15          # ロード: the slot list shows "1 棋譜1 .入門"
wait 400
shot saved
