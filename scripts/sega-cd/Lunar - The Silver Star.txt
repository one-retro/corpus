# Lunar - The Silver Star: start a new game, walk out of the opening
# scenes to Dyne's Memorial, open the menu, switch to Nall (SAVE / LOAD /
# ORDER / DROP) and save to slot 1. The game's file takes 40 blocks of
# internal backup RAM. Genesis A is RetroPad y here; C (RetroPad a) opens the menu.
wait 600
press start 6       # BIOS
wait 1500
press start 6       # skip the logos
wait 300
press start 6
wait 350
press start 6       # skip the intro
wait 300
press a 6           # title, START / CONTINUE, the file list
wait 200
press a 6           # (C confirms; 0: RESTART GAME starts a new game)
wait 300
press a 6
wait 300
press a 6
wait 600            # "North of the village of Burg..."
repeat 7            # opening text and scenes
tap a 10 40
end
wait 1200           # the Alex and Nall scene runs longer in Genesis Plus GX Wide
press b 6
wait 100
shot field
repeat 4            # Nall: "Alex, didn't you promise Luna..." (B pages text; spare
press b 6           # presses do nothing in the field)
wait 80
end
wait 100
press a 6           # menu: MAGIC EQUIP ITEM STATUS AI
wait 100
press right 6       # Nall: SAVE LOAD ORDER DROP
wait 100
shot nall
press y 6           # SAVE
wait 150
press y 6           # 1: NOT USED
wait 200
shot saved
