# Robot Ponkots - Sun Version - Comic Bom Bom Special Version (Japan) (HuC-1 +
# 32 KiB battery RAM, infrared port): the Japanese original of Robopon Sun,
# and the same play as scripts/gbc/Robopon Sun.txt: mash A through the opening
# to the grandfather's lab, walk right, away from him, then start menu ->
# セーブ -> "セーブしました" (saved).
wait 1200
repeat 300          # title, New Game, the opening, name and birthday, the lab;
tap a 1 40          # surplus presses only replay Grandpa's lines
end
repeat 16           # A ends his lines; RIGHT is ignored while he talks and
tap a 1 40          # walks away from him once he stops, so the next A has
press right 10      # no one to talk to
wait 10
end
wait 30
press start 6       # ロボポン / もちもの / そうこ / データ / せってい / セーブ
wait 120            # (the menu fades in)
repeat 5
press down 6
wait 20
end
press a 6           # セーブ: written
wait 60
press a 6           # (harmless if nothing asks)
wait 120
shot saved          # "セーブしました"
wait 30
