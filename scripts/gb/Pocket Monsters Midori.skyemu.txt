# Pocket Monsters Midori (Japan) (MBC1 + 32 KiB battery RAM, banked): the same
# play as Pokemon Red: さいしょからはじめる, the first preset names (グリーン and
# the rival's), then レポート (save) from the start menu in the bedroom.
# Variant for skyemu: SkyEmu's reset wipes battery RAM, so the proof is the saved message
# rather than the title's つづきから after a reset.
# B advances Oak's text but never picks from the name menus, so it is mashed.
wait 1500
repeat 8            # START opens the title menu and START picks さいしょから; the
tap start 1 50      # title ignores input while its Pokemon swap, and SGB-mode
end                 # cores (mGBA, Gearboy) reach it ~300 frames later
wait 60
repeat 60           # Oak's speech, stops at the player's name menu
tap b 1 30
end
press down 4        # the first preset name
wait 20
press a 4
wait 60
repeat 40           # stops at the rival's name menu
tap b 1 30
end
press down 4        # the first preset rival name
wait 20
press a 4
wait 60
repeat 40           # the rest of the speech; B does nothing in the bedroom
tap b 1 30
end
wait 60
press start 6       # ポケモン / どうぐ / グリーン / レポート / せってい / とじる
wait 60
press down 4
wait 15
press down 4
wait 15
press down 4
wait 15
press a 6           # レポート -> write the report?
wait 180
press a 6           # はい
wait 140            # this core's battery RAM starts zeroed, so no "overwrite?"
shot saved          # "グリーンは レポートに しっかり かきのこした!"
wait 200
