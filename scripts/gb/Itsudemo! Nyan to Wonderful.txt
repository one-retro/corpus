# Itsudemo! Nyan to Wonderful (Japan) (MBC3 + clock + 8 KiB battery RAM): a
# virtual pet that runs on the cartridge clock. さいしょから (new game), keep
# the clock at AM 00:00, 出会い (meet), take the first puppy, name it "ア",
# confirm: the game writes the pet and the clock it set.
wait 1200
press start 6       # title: さいしょから
wait 120
press a 6           # 現在の時間は? AM 00:00 -> けってい
wait 120
press a 6           # main menu: 出会い
wait 120
press a 6           # どの子にしますか? -> the first puppy
wait 120
press a 8           # なまえは? "ア"
wait 40
press up 8          # wraps to the bottom row
wait 30
press left 8        # wraps to おわり
wait 30
press a 8           # おわり -> この子でよろしいですか? いいえ / はい
wait 120
press a 8           # はい (the default)
wait 200
shot registered     # back on the main menu with the puppy registered
wait 60
