# Pokemon Red (MBC3 + 32 KiB battery RAM, no clock): NEW GAME, take the
# preset names RED and BLUE, then SAVE from the start menu in the bedroom.
# B advances Oak's text but never picks from the name menus, so it is mashed.
wait 1500
repeat 8            # START opens the title menu and START picks NEW GAME; the
tap start 1 50      # title ignores input while its Pokemon swap, and SGB-mode
end                 # cores (mGBA, Gearboy) reach it ~300 frames later
wait 60
repeat 60           # Oak's speech, stops at the player's name menu
tap b 1 30
end
press down 4        # RED
wait 20
press a 4
wait 60
repeat 40           # stops at the rival's name menu
tap b 1 30
end
press down 4        # BLUE
wait 20
press a 4
wait 60
repeat 40           # the rest of the speech; B does nothing in the bedroom
tap b 1 30
end
wait 60
press start 6       # POKEMON / ITEM / RED / SAVE / OPTION / EXIT
wait 60
press down 4
wait 15
press down 4
wait 15
press down 4
wait 15
press a 6           # SAVE -> "Would you like to SAVE the game?"
wait 180
press a 6           # YES
wait 210
shot saved          # "RED saved the game!"
wait 120
