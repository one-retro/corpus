# Mario Kart DS: on the first SINGLE PLAYER the game asks to confirm the
# nickname (the firmware user name), confirms how it shows in races, and
# offers a custom emblem; answering writes the player profile to flash.
# Then SINGLE PLAYER again goes straight to SELECT MODE.
wait 700            # main menu, before the attract demo starts
press a 6           # SINGLE PLAYER
wait 240
shot nickname       # "Is the nickname Player correct?"
press a 6           # YES
wait 240
shot onscreen       # "During races, Player will appear on-screen. Is this OK?"
press a 6           # YES
wait 240
shot emblem         # "Create a custom emblem?" (cursor on NO)
press a 6           # NO: the profile is saved here
wait 300
press a 6           # SINGLE PLAYER again
wait 300
shot selectmode     # SELECT MODE, no questions this time
wait 2400           # melonDS writes its .sav 2 s (wall clock) after the game's last write
