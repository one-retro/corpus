# Tetris DS: SINGLE PLAYER, STANDARD, MARATHON at level 1, then hard-drop
# (Up) every piece until the stack tops out. The game writes the new high
# score on GAME OVER. TRY AGAIN starts a new game whose HIGH SCORE shows it.
wait 1200
press start 6       # title -> SINGLE PLAYER
wait 200
press a 6           # SINGLE PLAYER
wait 200
press a 6           # STANDARD
wait 200
press a 6           # MARATHON -> level 1
wait 200
press a 6           # level
wait 60
press right 6       # OK
wait 60
press a 6
wait 300
repeat 60           # hard drops until the stack tops out
press up 4
wait 30
end
wait 300
shot gameover       # GAME OVER, the score
press a 6           # TRY AGAIN
wait 300
shot highscore      # new game: HIGH SCORE is the score just saved
wait 2400           # melonDS writes its .sav 2 s (wall clock) after the game's last write
