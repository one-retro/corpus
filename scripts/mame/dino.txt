# Cadillacs and Dinosaurs (CPS1 with QSound; settings in a 93C46 EEPROM): enter the
# service menu, SYSTEM CONFIGURATION: COIN becomes 1 COIN 2 CREDITS and CONTINUE
# OFF, then EXIT, which writes the EEPROM. Power-cycle and open the menu again to
# show the values read back. The service switch is F2 on the keyboard in the MAME
# cores; FinalBurn Neo and FB Alpha open the menu when START is held (their
# "Diagnostic Input" default), see dino.finalburn-neo.txt / dino.fb-alpha.txt.
# Button 1 is RetroPad b.
wait 900
key f2 10              # TEST MENU
wait 120
tap down 4 30          # 5: SYSTEM CONFIGURATION
press b 6
wait 90
press b 6              # 1 COIN: 1 COIN 2 CREDITS
wait 60
tap down 1 30          # 2 CONTINUE
tap right 1 30         # OFF
shot config
repeat 10              # down to 12 EXIT
tap down 1 30
end
press b 6              # EXIT: PRESS 1P START TO RETURN TO MAIN MENU
wait 60
press start 6           # back to the TEST MENU: the EEPROM is written
wait 120
shot menu
repeat 6               # TEST MENU: 5 -> 11 EXIT
tap down 1 30
end
press b 6
wait 600
# power cycle, back into SYSTEM CONFIGURATION
reset
wait 900
key f2 10
wait 120
tap down 4 30
press b 6
wait 120
shot saved             # 1 COIN 2 CREDITS, CONTINUE OFF
