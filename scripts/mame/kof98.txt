# The King of Fighters '98 (MVS, backup RAM), MAME cores: the test switch (F2 on the
# keyboard) opens the MVS BIOS menu.
# SETTING UP THE SOFT DIP: the cabinet's CONTINUE becomes 2 COINS = 1 CREDIT, and
# K.O.F.'98's own PLAY TIME becomes LITTLE FAST and CONTINUE OFF. The BIOS keeps
# these in backup RAM. A power cycle then shows them read back.
# Neo Geo A (RetroPad b) changes a value / opens, C (RetroPad y) goes back.
wait 1500
key f2 10              # test switch: BIOS menu
wait 300
tap down 2 30          # SETTING UP THE SOFT DIP
press b 6
wait 60
press b 6              # SETTING UP THE CABINET
wait 60
tap down 1 30
press b 6              # CONTINUE 2COINS=1CREDIT
wait 60
shot cabinet
press y 6              # back
wait 60
tap down 1 30
press b 6              # SLOT1 K.O.F.'98
wait 60
press b 6              # PLAY TIME: LITTLE FAST
wait 60
tap down 1 30
press b 6              # CONTINUE: OFF
wait 60
shot kof98
press y 6              # back to the slot list
wait 60
press y 6              # back to the menu
wait 120
press y 6              # (and leave the menu)
wait 120
tap down 4 30          # EXIT
press b 6
wait 600
# power cycle and look again
reset
wait 1500
key f2 10
wait 300
tap down 2 30
press b 6
wait 60
tap down 1 30
press b 6              # SLOT1 K.O.F.'98
wait 120
shot saved             # PLAY TIME LITTLE FAST, CONTINUE OFF
