# Load script for a downloaded Time Trial ghost file (GameFAQs) of Mario Kart: Double Dash!!,
# Yoshi Circuit. A ghost is its own card file; the game's main data is not on the card, so the

# LOAD GHOST DATA, which lists the ghost found in Slot A.
# libretro Dolphin under retro-run, single-core (scripts/gc/dolphin-single-core.opt).

wait 300

wait 300

wait 90

wait 300
press a 6

press a 6
wait 300

wait 300

wait 90

wait 300

wait 90

wait 90

wait 300

wait 300
stick max 0 8
wait 90
stick max 0 8      # Star Cup
wait 90
press a 6

stick 0 max 8
wait 90
stick 0 max 8      # Yoshi Circuit
wait 90
