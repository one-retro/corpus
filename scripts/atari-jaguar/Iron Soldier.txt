# Iron Soldier: set DIFFICULTY from MEDIUM to HARD in OPTIONS and quit
# them. The game keeps its options (and mission progress) in the cartridge
# EEPROM, and writes it on QUIT. Then reset and open OPTIONS again: HARD
# comes back from the EEPROM.
wait 900
press start 6       # skip the intro
wait 300
press b 6           # title picture
wait 200
press b 6           # -> START GAME / LOAD GAME / OPTIONS / DEMO
wait 200
press down 6
wait 40
press down 6        # OPTIONS
wait 40
press b 6
wait 200
repeat 6            # the cursor starts on QUIT; up to DIFFICULTY
press up 6
wait 40
end
press right 6       # MEDIUM -> HARD
wait 40
repeat 6            # back down to QUIT
press down 6
wait 40
end
shot changed
press b 6           # QUIT: the EEPROM is written here
wait 200
reset
wait 900
press start 6
wait 300
press b 6
wait 200
press b 6
wait 200
press down 6
wait 40
press down 6
wait 40
press b 6
wait 200
shot reloaded
