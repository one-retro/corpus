# Doom: pick Ultra-Violence in the start menu and begin a game. Doom keeps
# its start menu (skill, area), volumes and controls in the cartridge
# EEPROM and writes them when a game starts. Then reset: the menu comes
# back with Ultra-Violence, read from the EEPROM.
wait 900            # the attract demo is running by now
press b 6           # -> start menu (Game Mode / Area / Difficulty)
wait 120
press down 6        # Difficulty
wait 30
press right 6       # Hurt me plenty -> Ultra-Violence
wait 30
press b 6           # start the game: the EEPROM is written here
wait 400
shot playing
reset
wait 900
press b 6
wait 120
shot reloaded
